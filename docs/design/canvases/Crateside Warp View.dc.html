Each stem's warp, on Track details

One time axis for the group, under the sample list: a lane per stem, with Live's clip-view parts drawn in place. Flags above the waveform for markers, grey ticks under it for transients, a bar along the top for the loop, and everything outside the clip dimmed. Above the lanes, a tempo line read from the markers. It is a flat line for a two-marker warp and a steady wobble for a dense one, so both read at a glance. The lanes share one axis, so stems that agree line up, and a stem warped on its own shows where it parts from the rest. Click a lane to see its details, where it came from, and the two controls. Everything is monochrome except state: amber for waiting for Live (your turn), teal while crateside writes.

Waveforms, markers and dates are made up. Rapper's Delight's range is the one DESIGN.md records. The lanes, pills and controls are live: click them.

1fZoom, and room to grow

Zoom earns its place, as three fixed steps: Whole track, 32 bars and 8 bars, counted from the shared warp. Zoomed, a strip of the whole track appears above the ruler with the view boxed in it: click to move, or use ‹ ›. Transients are drawn only from 32 bars, where they sit 3px or more apart. Markers are drawn as flags wherever two are at least 9px apart. Closer than that, they become ticks in the flag row with faint lines through the lane, so a dense warp reads as a texture and its bunching still shows. See 1b whole and 1c at 8 bars.

Editing, later. The flags are already the handles: in an editing version they drag along their lane, a click in the flag row adds one, and the clip-start notch in the ruler drags to bar 1 of the first verse. Edits would apply to the group, with a stem warped on its own edited on its own lane. 8 bars is the zoom an edit would need.

Off the grid, #96. The 10 s offsets get a lane of their own between the tempo line and the stems: Off the grid, milliseconds either side of a centre line, on the same axis. A step like the 50 ms one at 4:40 would show directly above the markers that absorbed it, and the offer to correct it would sit in the detail, beside the two controls.
