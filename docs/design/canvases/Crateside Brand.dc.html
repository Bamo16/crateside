1crateside — mark and wordmark

Constraints I set. It has to work at 18px in the header's brand cell, at 16px as a favicon, and at 64px as an app tile. Same palette as the app: ink on --surface, one accent, no gradients. Type is the app's type — IBM Plex — so the wordmark is the word the header already shows, made deliberate. Marks are geometry only, one stroke weight, so they survive the sizes.

Amber, not teal. In the app amber means "your turn". The brand is the thing you look at when it is your turn, so the accent is amber everywhere — teal would claim the machine is working.

Five directions, one image each. 1a the side of a crate, 1b a record in a crate, 1c a warp marker, 1d a groove monogram, 1e type only. Each shown as lockup, in the header cell at its built geometry (BrandNav.razor.css: max-content width, 18/22 padding, 15px/600 brand, 12px/500 tabs, amber underline), and as tiles at 64/32/16. Tab names are the four pages in the repo.
