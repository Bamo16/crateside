Spend the row's width, not its height

At 92rem the samples column is about 1100px wide, and its lines are mostly empty space. In the row, the side stack becomes two columns, so it is about as tall as a short list. While picking, the stack folds away (it is inert then anyway) and the presets lay out in bands side by side, so the open picker is about as tall as the closed list. Expanding or opening a row moves the rows below by a similar amount in every state. No cap and no inner scroll.
